How to use this viewer:

  1. Export a chat from WhatsApp (with or without media).
  2. If the export is a .zip file, extract it to a folder on your computer.
  3. Click the Import Chat & Media button at the top right.
  4. Highlight the .txt file AND all the images/videos at the exact same time (you can drag to select or use Ctrl+A / Cmd+A) and click Open.

🔒 Your data remains completely private. Everything is processed locally on this device and is never uploaded to any server.
